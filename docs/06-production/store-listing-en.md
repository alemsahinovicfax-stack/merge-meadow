## Google Play

### Short description (max 80 znakova)

```
Run, collect, merge! Cute lane runner + cozy camp upgrades. Free to play.
```

*(72 znaka)*

### Full description

```
Merge Meadow blends quick arcade runs with satisfying merge upgrades in a cozy pastel world.

🏃 RUN — Sprint through 3-lane meadows in 60-second sessions. Swipe left and right, grab coins and seeds, dodge obstacles, and chase a bigger haul every time.

🌸 MERGE — Return to your camp between runs. Plant seeds, merge matching flowers (2→1), and watch your garden grow. Every merge pops with satisfying feedback.

⚡ GROW — Upgrade your magnet, equip a seed basket for bonus spawns, and unlock stronger runs as you clear 100 campaign levels or push Endless mode.

✨ FAIR FREE TO PLAY
• Core loop is always free — no energy gates
• Optional rewarded videos for double loot or a second chance
• Remove interstitial ads anytime with a one-time purchase
• Starter pack for a cozy boost — no loot boxes, no gacha

Perfect for coffee-break sessions that still feel rewarding tomorrow.

Download Merge Meadow — run today, merge tomorrow.
```
