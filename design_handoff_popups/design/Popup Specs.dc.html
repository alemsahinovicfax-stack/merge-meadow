Pop-ups — one system for the whole game
Every pop-up is a cream sticker with an ink rim and one hard shadow — the same material as the field tiles, Shop cards and the Wardrobe. The five kinds differ by shape, so a player knows how each one closes before reading a word: a modal wears a title plate on its top edge, a sheet has a handle, a coach bubble has a tail, a toast is a dark pill, a pop is a number with an ink outline.
