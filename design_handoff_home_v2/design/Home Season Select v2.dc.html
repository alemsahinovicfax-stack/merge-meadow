Rules
Swipe on the card changes season. It never passes through to the hub, even at the first or last season (a rubber-band of 40 px instead), so the gesture is predictable.
Swipe anywhere else (header, dock, Play row, footer) changes hub page. Horizontal drags on the dock go to the hub; taps on the dock jump seasons.
The gesture is visible: there are always ‹ › arrows on the art, and the dock shows where you are.
Far locks can't take focus. A tap shakes the token and shows Unlock Lantern Meadow first. The pager skips them.
Why the Browser is gone
SeasonBrowser is the dock: 8 tokens, free and premium labelled, always on screen, one tap to any season you're allowed to focus. It's the same dark well plus cream rim as the chrome, so it reads as part of the scene and not as a debug menu. The old season_browser.tscn can be deleted.
