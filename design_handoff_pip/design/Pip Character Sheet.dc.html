Who Pip is
A young meadow rabbit who looks after the field. Curious first, brave second, a little dramatic when poked. Pip notices everything: a new flower gets a gasp, a butterfly gets a sneeze, the moon gets a long stare. Never sad for long.
Shape language
Circles and soft pears, never sharp except three places: the forehead tuft, the cheek fluff points, the folded ear crease. Head 1.15× the body width. Ears 0.7 of head height. Feet bigger than paws — rabbits stand on their feet.
Line & cel
Ink #2D3436, 3 px at the 256 frame (2.2 px at 190). Three tones per material: fur / fur_sh (light from top-left, shade lower-right) / fur_lt (muzzle, belly, tail). Eyes: iris rim + ink + two highlights. No gradients, blur, glow, masks.
How Pip moves
Every move has anticipation (crouch before a hop), squash on landing, stretch on take-off. Ears and the folded tip follow 1–2 frames late and overshoot. Real rabbit vocabulary: hop, binky, flop, thump, periscope, grooming, nose twitch.
