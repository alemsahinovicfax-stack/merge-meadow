Merge Meadow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version

  • You do not need an account. Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not sell your data.
  • The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID and similar data. Where the law requires it, we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Merge Meadow (“the game”) is a casual mobile game made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the processing described in this policy that we control. Google and Apple are responsible for the data they process under their own policies. You can reach us at mergemeadow.support@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed and why

Data Where it goes Why
Game progress and settings (coins, seeds, flowers, unlocked seasons, sound and vibration settings) Stored only on your device. Not sent to us. To save your game.
Advertising data: advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), app set ID, IP address, device and operating-system information, and how you interact with ads Google AdMob To show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you agree, personalise them.
Purchase records (which item was bought and a purchase token) Google Play or Apple; the token is also kept on your device To deliver what you bought, restore it and prevent double delivery.
Your email address and message, if you contact support Our support mailbox To answer you.

3. Advertising and your choices

The free version of the game shows rewarded ads (you choose to watch them, for example to double your loot) and may show an occasional ad at the end of a run. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google's use of data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before personalised ads are shown, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • United States: some states treat targeted advertising as “selling” or “sharing” personal information. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy choices and through your device settings.
  • On any device: you can reset or delete your advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads), or deny tracking (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Buying Remove Ads turns off ads that you did not choose to watch.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (for example Remove Ads, premium seasons, Pip skins and boosters) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their terms. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. We receive a purchase confirmation so the game can unlock the item, and we keep a purchase token on your device so the same purchase is not delivered twice. Restore purchases asks the store again for items you already own.

5. What we do not collect

  • No account, name, email or password is needed to play.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own. If we add any in the future, we will update this policy first.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Consent — personalised advertising and storing or reading advertising identifiers where consent is required.
  • Contract — delivering items you buy.
  • Legitimate interests — showing non-personalised ads to fund the free game, preventing ad fraud, and answering your support emails.

7. How long data is kept

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the game or clear its storage.
  • Advertising data is kept by Google according to its own retention policies.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you, and no longer than 12 months after the conversation ends.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because your game data stays on your device, you can delete it yourself by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. For advertising data, use Settings → Privacy choices, your device's ad settings, or Google's tools. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Merge Meadow is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Google and Apple may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. They use safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

11. Security

We do not run servers that store your data. Your game save stays in the game's private storage on your device. Ad and payment providers protect the data they process under their own security programmes.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game uses data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date, and where the change is important, tell you in the game.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy or the game: mergemeadow.support@gmail.com
